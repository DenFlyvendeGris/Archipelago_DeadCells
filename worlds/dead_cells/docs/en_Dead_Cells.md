# Dead Cells Archipelago

## What is this game?

Dead Cells is a roguelite metroidvania developed by Motion Twin and Evil Empire.
Fight through procedurally generated biomes, collect runes to unlock new paths,
and battle your way to the Hand of the King — or beyond.

Ported to Archipelago by DenFlyvendeGris, this World provides the following as unlockable items:

- Runes (Vine, Teleportation, Ram, Spider, Homunculus, and more)
- Boss Stem Cells
- Weapon and Skill Blueprints
- Scrolls (Power, Dual, Cursed)
- Gold Pouches
- Trap Items (Curse Trap, Malaise Trap)

### Goals

Three goal options are available, configured in your YAML:

- **Hand of the King** *(default)*: Defeat the Hand of the King. No DLC required.
  This is the most accessible goal and can be completed without collecting any runes,
  making it suitable for new players or short sessions.

- **The Collector**: Defeat the Collector, the true final boss. Requires the
  Rise of the Giant DLC. Expects a full progression chain through runes and biomes —
  recommended for experienced players.

- **Target BSC**: Collect a set number of Boss Stem Cells (1–5). A flexible goal
  suitable for any experience level depending on the target chosen.

### DLC Support

The following DLC packs are supported and can be toggled individually in your YAML:

- Rise of the Giant
- The Bad Seed
- Fatal Falls
- The Queen and the Sea

### Location Checks

Checks are awarded for:

- Defeating bosses
- Opening timed door chests (Bronze, Silver, Gold)
- Collecting fixed blueprint pickups
- Defeating each elite enemy type for the first time (permanent across runs)
- No-hit doors after boss fights
